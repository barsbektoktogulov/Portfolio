# Student Warning System


As School President, I introduced and implemented the school's first student warning system to improve discipline and promote a more respectful learning environment.

At the beginning of each academic quarter, a list of students' names was displayed in every classroom. Teachers could issue warnings to students for misconduct, such as disrupting lessons or engaging in physical altercations. Students who received more than three warnings in one quarter were subject to expulsion.

The system proved effective in improving student discipline. During the first quarter, nearly every class recorded at least four to five warnings. By the fourth and final quarter, the number had decreased to a maximum of one warning per class, demonstrating a significant improvement in student behavior
